Privacy by design, and a journey that feeds partners

Data privacy & security choices

ConsentPlain-language notice in the owner’s language before KYC: what, why, how long, rights. KYC consent is required; WhatsApp updates and marketing are separate, and marketing is off by default. Versioned and timestamped in a consent ledger.
Purpose limitationKYC data is used only for verification and activation. The advisor’s profile never enters the KYC record; the propensity model uses no names or identifiers.
Data minimisationDocuments are OCR’d in memory and the image is discarded straight away. Only the extracted fields are kept, and those are dropped once the order is placed. GSTIN, Udyam no., phone and email are redacted before any text reaches the AI model.
MaskingGSTIN 27XXXXXXXXXU1ZL, Udyam XXXX547, IDs, names and phones are masked in the UI, the back office and the logs (automatic log scrubber). Mobile numbers are stored as a hash plus the last 4 digits.
Retention & rightsVerified details are kept for the contract plus the statutory period. A one-tap “Delete my KYC data” button (right to erasure) and a grievance officer contact sit on the consent screen.
SecurityImage-only uploads up to 5 MB, security headers, back office behind a role key (SSO/RBAC in production), sessions expire after 2 h idle, encryption at rest and in transit in production.

Field force & channel partners

  • Assist, don’t chase: “Talk to an expert” creates a hand-off with the full profile and quote, so a partner never asks the same questions twice
  • Partners keep the commission on digital closes in their PIN codes, so the app is their lead engine, not a competitor
  • Call list by propensity × value: field sales only for multi-site, manufacturers and high-value drops
  • Partners own the last mile: site survey, installation and the first-week check-in; the tracker shows their progress live

KYC answer quality on the specimen pack

Blur/unreadable (K02, K04) · cancelled GSTIN + blank Udyam no. (K01) · GSTIN typo caught by checksum + nearest registry match (K06, K10) · legal-name mismatch (K07) · bill older than 90 days (K11) · “Owner” as signatory for LLP/Pvt Ltd (K05, K10, K11) · 4 clean files approved in under a minute.

Risks & mitigation

  • AI suggests the wrong product → rules engine is final, advisor scenario regression suite run on every change
  • KYC fraud / tampered docs → registry match + checksum; manual review queue on any fail
  • Partner pushback → PIN-code attribution and commission on digital closes
  • Fibre not feasible → auto-switch to 5G AirFiber, feasibility check before payment
